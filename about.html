About The House Of Wisteria

The House Of Wisteria is a modern luxury hotel that combines comfort, elegance, and exceptional hospitality. Designed for business travelers, families, and tourists, our hotel provides a peaceful and memorable stay with premium facilities and personalized service.

Our hotel features beautifully designed rooms, a spacious lobby, a fine dining restaurant, a relaxing spa, a swimming pool, landscaped gardens, and elegant ballrooms for weddings, conferences, and celebrations. Every guest is welcomed with warmth and treated with the highest level of care.
